Privacy Policy
Chess Duel

Effective: November 19, 2026 · Last Updated: November 19, 2026 · Jurisdiction: India
📋 This policy explains how FunMindo Studio ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile game Chess Duel ("the Game"). By using the Game you agree to it. Our Terms of Service also apply.
1

Summary

Chess Duel does not ask you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, phone number or contact list. Your games, ratings and settings stay on your device. The only data that leaves your device is what is needed for online play, ads and purchases, handled by Google services as described below.

3

How we use it

  • To run the game and save your progress on your device
  • To connect two players in an online game
  • To show ads, and to remove them for subscribers
  • To keep the app working and fix crashes
  • To follow the law

We do not sell your data and do not build profiles about you.

4

Online play (Google Firebase)

Online play uses a short room code and an anonymous Firebase sign-in, so no name or email is needed. Moves and game requests are kept only while the game exists. Anyone who has your room code can join that game, so share it only with the person you want to play. Offline modes (computer, two players on one phone, puzzles, training) do not use Firebase.

5

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows banner, interstitial and rewarded video ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use your Advertising ID to show ads and measure them. Chess Duel is for ages 13 and above and is not directed at children.

  • Where required (EEA, UK, Switzerland) a Google consent form lets you choose whether ads are personalised
  • You can opt out of personalised ads or reset your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads
  • Subscribers do not see ads
6

Subscriptions

Chess Duel offers optional weekly and monthly subscriptions that remove ads. Payment, billing and refunds are handled by Google Play. We only learn whether a subscription is active. Manage or cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

7

Notifications, voice and sharing

  • Daily reminders are optional local notifications created on your phone. They need your permission and can be turned off in Settings. No server is involved.
  • Voice commentary uses your phone's built-in text-to-speech. Nothing is recorded.
  • Sharing an invite or result opens your phone's share sheet only when you tap it.
  • Feedback opens your email app; nothing is sent unless you send it.
  • Chess Duel does not use your camera, microphone, contacts or location.
8

Children

Chess Duel is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has given us information, email us and we will act on it.

9

Retention and deletion

  • Game data stays on your device until you clear the app data or uninstall Chess Duel
  • Online room data is kept only for the life of the game
  • Data held by Google (ads, purchases, diagnostics) follows Google's own policies at policies.google.com/privacy

We hold no personal account data, so there is nothing for us to delete. For any request, email us.

10

Security and transfers

We use Google Firebase access rules and standard Google services to protect online data, but no system is perfectly secure. Google may process data outside India, including in the United States.

11

Your rights

Under Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, you may ask what data we hold, ask for correction or deletion, withdraw consent, and make a complaint. Email funmindostudio@gmail.com; we reply within 30 days. You can also withdraw consent by uninstalling the game.

12

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Continued use after a change means you accept it.
